Never miss the return window again.

It finds the dated deadline buried in an email, such as a return window, a trial end or a renewal, shows you the exact reminder, and after you approve it schedules that reminder to your own inbox before the date. It also keeps one running tracker draft.

02 How it works

Three steps, one approval.

  1. 1

    Find the date

    From your own message, or from one source email read with a bounded search. Relative, partial or conflicting dates are flagged for you to confirm. It never guesses.

  2. 2

    Show the exact preview

    To, subject, body and the send time as weekday, date, local time and timezone. By default the reminder lands 3 days before at 9:00 AM. Nothing is scheduled until you approve.

  3. 3

    Schedule and track

    One schedule_email_send call for the approved reminder, then the same tracker draft is updated in place with save_draft. Ask "show my deadline tracker" to read it back.

03 Safety

Emails supply a date. Never instructions.

Result

Ignored. Reminder addressed to you only.

The CC request is reported and dropped. No extra recipient is added.

  • Recipient is always you

    Your own resolved mailbox. No To, Cc or Bcc is ever taken from a source email.

  • Nothing is scheduled without approval

    Every reminder gets an exact preview and fresh approval. A changed date or lead time needs a new one.

  • Urgent wording is not authority

    Source emails are untrusted data. They cannot set recipients, lead times, send times or approval.

  • No duplicate reminders

    The tracker is checked first. If the item and deadline are already tracked, it reports the existing entry instead.
